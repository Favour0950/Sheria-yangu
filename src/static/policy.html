Terms & data policy

What we collect

Your phone number is never stored as plain text — only a one-way cryptographic hash of it, keyed with a secret pepper only the server holds. No one, including Sheria Yangu's own team, can reverse that hash back into a phone number.

What "pseudonymous" means here

Your votes and memoranda are linked to a randomly generated token, not to your phone number or identity directly. That token is technically reversible only by whoever controls both the identity table and the server-side pepper — deliberately walled off, minimized, and not touched by ordinary app code (like analytics).

Constituency — self-reported, not verified

The constituency you enter is not checked against the voter roll or any national ID system — Sheria Yangu doesn't have access to that data. County participation analytics reflect what people typed in, not confirmed residency.

What county analytics can see

Only a count of votes per constituency. It never queries your identity, phone number, or token — it reads only the anonymous, point-in-time constituency snapshot taken when you voted.

Sending a memorandum

Sheria Yangu never sends anything to Parliament or a county assembly on your behalf automatically. It drafts a memorandum for you to review and edit, then opens your own email app (or gives you the text and address to paste into your own webmail) so you send it yourself. We only record that you did.

One memorandum per bill

You can send one memorandum per bill — this stops the same objection being submitted multiple times by mistake. You can still redraft and edit before you actually send.

Notifications

SMS, email, and in-app notifications are all opt-in, controlled in Settings. Turning them off stops future notifications; it doesn't delete your past votes or memoranda.
